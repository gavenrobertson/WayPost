## Routes: Polestar Guides

The 1-60 leveling routes for both factions, and the class, profession, reputation, exploration and
collection guides, are converted from **Polestar Guides** by The Polestar authors
(<https://github.com/fitzforge/polestar>), licensed GPL-3.0. `tools/import_polestar.py` reads
Polestar's compiled guides and writes them in Waypost's format; the guide text (step titles, tips,
notes) is Polestar's, used under the GPL-3.0. Fixes to a route belong upstream in Polestar first.

Thank you to Fitz and every Polestar contributor.

## Reference data in the routes

Quest names, NPCs, objects, items and coordinates in those routes were resolved by Polestar from the
sources listed in `Waypost_Routes_*/THIRD-PARTY-DATA.txt` (Polestar's own notice file, shipped
unchanged), among them the pfQuest database by Shagu (MIT), CMaNGOS classic-db (GPL-3.0), Efficient
Games' EG Link output (CC BY 4.0) and Blizzard client tables via wago.tools, used as facts.

## Talent plans

`Waypost/Data/Talents.lua` is converted from Polestar's talent plans, which follow ForeverDB's
leveling builds (<https://foreverdb.net>) and Polestar's own extensions.

## Not used

Nothing in Waypost comes from RestedXP's guides, free or paid. Its route content is not open to
reuse under the GPL and none of it was copied, retyped or paraphrased.

## Art

The 3D arrow, logo and map pins are original and generated by `tools/make_arrow3d.py` and
`tools/make_art.py`. Every other texture is the game's own interface art, referenced by path.
