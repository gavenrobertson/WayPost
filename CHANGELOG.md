# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Layouts for the guide window: Simple (just what to do and where), Standard, Detailed (details
  under every goal, finished goals, why the step is there, five steps ahead, a live XP bar), or
  Custom: tick the parts you want in Settings. Switch from the ... menu or `/waypost layout`.
- Resizing: drag the guide window's corner to change its width and the step list's bottom edge to
  show more steps. Every other window resizes from its corner and remembers its size;
  double-click a corner to reset it.
- Live XP tracker (`/waypost tracker`, or click the XP text in the guide window): XP per hour over
  the last ten minutes that rises and falls as you play, time to level counting down, XP for each
  of the last ten minutes, quest and kill XP for the session, about how many kills or quests are
  left to level, and a "+N" on each gain.
- The Waypost button now sits inside the game menu (Esc), in the menu's own list. Click for
  settings, right-click for the guide list.
- Settings has Guides and Help buttons at the top.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Leveling routes 1-60 for every Alliance and Horde race (65 guides), plus 194 class, profession,
  reputation, exploration and collection guides, converted from Polestar Guides (GPL-3.0).
- Guide window with a "Now" card (what, who, where, live distance and direction), the step's other
  goals, upcoming steps, full step list, compact mode, three window styles.
- A Waypost button inside the game menu (Esc), in the menu's own list of buttons.
- Guide window layouts: Simple, Standard, Detailed, or Custom from any mix of parts. Every window
  resizes from its corner and remembers its size.
- Live XP tracker: XP per hour over a sliding ten-minute window, time to level counting down, XP
  per minute, quest and kill XP, kills or quests to level.
- 3D navigation arrow with distance and time to arrive; world map and minimap pins; TomTom hand-off.
- Auto accept and turn-in for guide quests, quest item and target buttons with key bindings,
  flight point tracking and auto-flight.
- Skip, undo, look ahead, "why this step", danger warnings, safer-route and XP-skip options.
- Later: quests set aside and offered back when you group up or gain levels; a catch-up card at
  level checkpoints.
- Welcome window on first login and an in-game help window.
- XP per hour, level and guide timers, a chart of the time each level took, run history with time
  saved.
- Level-up animation in the guide window.
- Talent guide, gear upgrade hints (Pawn supported), orphaned-quest cleanup.
- Party sync, guild leveling board, guild route and officer announcements.
- Custom guides: plain-text format, in-game editor, import/export strings, web editor, route recorder.
- Progress export for dragonwarriors.app with a companion uploader.
